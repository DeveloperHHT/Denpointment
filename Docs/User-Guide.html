1Getting started

Denpointment runs on your own computer. You need Python 3.9 or newer. MySQL is only needed for real data; the demo mode works without it.

1

Install and start the demo

Open a terminal in the project folder and run these commands once. The demo opens on http://127.0.0.1:5000.

python3 -m venv .venv
.venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt
.venv/bin/python main.py --demo

For a real database, import Backend/Database/dentist_management_system.sql into MySQL, set the DENPOINTMENT_DB_* variables described in the README and start with python main.py.

2

Log in

The demo has four accounts, all with the password demo1234.

EmailWhat it shows
patient@denpointment.exampleA patient with an upcoming appointment and a history
patient2@denpointment.exampleA patient who can book an appointment right now
fresh@denpointment.exampleA new account that still has to add an address and a phone number
dentist@denpointment.exampleA dentist with appointments today, upcoming ones and holidays
3

Find your way around

The menu on the left shows what your role may use. At the bottom you find your name, the theme switch and the log out button. Messages appear in the top right corner and disappear by themselves.

2For patients

Everything a patient does, in the order you will meet it.

1

Create an account

Choose “Create an account” on the login page. Fill in your name, SSN (10 to 15 digits), email, gender, birth date and a password of at least six characters. Mistakes are shown under the field they belong to.

2

Activate your account

After the first login the dashboard asks for an address and a phone number. The account becomes active as soon as both exist, no extra button needed.

3

Fill in your profile

Open My profile. Use the Add buttons for addresses, phone numbers and chronic diseases, the pencil to edit and the red bin to delete (the system asks to confirm). You always keep at least one address and one phone number. Email and password are changed with the two buttons at the top.

4

Book an appointment

Open Book an appointment, then (1) choose a dentist, (2) pick a day, (3) pick a time and press Book appointment. The clinic works 08:00 to 12:00 and 15:00 to 16:00 (the last slot starts at 16:00).

Good to know. You can book only for the rest of the current month, only one upcoming appointment at a time, and not on a day the dentist is on holiday or at a time somebody else already booked.
5

Follow your appointments and treatments

My appointments lists what is coming and what happened before. For a finished visit press Details to see the treatment, the charge and the prescribed medicines.

3For dentists

Dentists use the “Clinic” part of the menu. They are listed in the database; there is no registration for dentists.

1

Start at the dashboard

The three tiles show today's appointments, the upcoming ones and a shortcut to your holidays.

2

See today's appointments

Each row shows the time, the patient and whether a treatment is already recorded. Patient opens the patient's profile, Add treatment records what you did.

3

Record a treatment

Type or pick the action and the complaint (the lists only suggest, you can write your own), the charge and an optional note. Treatments can only be recorded and edited on the day of the appointment.

4

Add medicines

Saving a treatment opens the medicine list. Add as many medicines as needed; the bin removes one again. The patient sees the list in their treatment details.

5

Look ahead

Upcoming lists everything booked after today. From each row you can open the patient profile (addresses, phone numbers, chronic diseases). You only see patients who have booked with you.

6

Search the treatment history

Treatment history shows every appointment before today with its treatment. Choose a date and press Search to see one day; Clear brings the full list back.

7

Plan your holidays

Holidays lists days off. Press Add a holiday, pick a date in the next month and give an optional reason. Patients cannot book you on those days.

Limits. At most three holiday days per month, only in the next month, and a holiday of the current month cannot be deleted any more.
8

Read the statistics

The statistics page gives totals, the busiest dentists, the most common complaints (by gender, this month and last month), the average charge per treatment and the most expensive treatment.

4The rules at a glance

The system refuses requests that break these rules and tells you why.

TopicRule
ActivationA person becomes a patient when the profile has at least one address and one phone number.
ProfileThe last address and the last phone number cannot be deleted. A phone number can belong to one person only.
BookingOnly for the rest of the current month, only one upcoming appointment, working hours only (08–12 and 15–16), not in the past.
SlotsA dentist cannot have two appointments at the same time, nor on a holiday.
TreatmentsRecorded and edited on the day of the appointment, by the dentist of that appointment. One treatment per appointment.
HolidaysNext month only, three days per month at most, deletable until the month begins.
Patient dataA dentist can open the profile of patients who have booked with them, nobody else's.
SecurityForms carry a CSRF token, delete actions are POST requests, passwords are stored hashed (sha256_crypt).

5Themes and small screens

The interface follows your system theme at first. The moon or sun button in the menu switches it and remembers your choice in this browser.

On a phone the menu hides behind the button at the top right and tables turn into cards.

6Troubleshooting

QuestionAnswer
“Your form has expired”The page was open for a long time or the server restarted. Reload the page and send the form again.
I cannot book an appointmentCheck that the profile has an address and a phone number, that you have no upcoming appointment and that the day is still in the current month.
The menu has no “Clinic” sectionOnly accounts that are in the dentists table see it.
The demo data looks oldmain.py --demo creates a fresh demo database on every start, with dates relative to today.
MySQL refuses the connectionCheck DENPOINTMENT_DB_HOST, _USER, _PASSWORD and _NAME. The MySQL path has not been run in the automated tests (they use SQLite).
